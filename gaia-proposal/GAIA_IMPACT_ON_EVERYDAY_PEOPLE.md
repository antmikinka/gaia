## What Is GAIA? (Simple Explanation)

GAIA is like having a **team of expert helpers** who can build software for you.

**Before GAIA:**
- You have an idea for an app
- You need to learn coding (months/years)
- Or hire developers ($10,000+)
- Most people give up

**After GAIA:**
- You have an idea for an app
- You type what you want in plain English
- GAIA builds it (30-50 minutes)
- You have a working app

**That's it. That's the revolution.**
